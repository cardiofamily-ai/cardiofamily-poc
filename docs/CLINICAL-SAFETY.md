# Clinical Safety — POC

CardioFamily POC is not a medical device and is not intended for clinical use.

All people, variants, results and events contained in the demonstration must
be synthetic.

Clinical-like actions are demonstration workflow examples.

Every action should clearly indicate:

"Demonstration only — requires clinician review."

The system must not claim to:
- diagnose disease
- prescribe treatment
- replace a cardiologist
- replace a clinical geneticist
- classify variants autonomously
- determine clinical management autonomously

If clinical rules are uncertain or have not been supplied, do not invent
authoritative recommendations.

Prefer neutral wording such as:

"Consider clinical review"

rather than:

"Patient requires treatment X."
