# CardioFamily Product Definition

## Vision

One cardiac genetic diagnosis should trigger appropriate care for the whole
family. CardioFamily helps make sure that it does.

## Problem

Inherited cardiovascular care is family-based, but workflows are often
patient-based and fragmented.

A genetic result may exist in one system, the family pedigree somewhere else,
testing status elsewhere, and surveillance information across multiple
clinical records.

Relatives can remain:
- untested
- unscreened
- overdue for surveillance
- disconnected from updated genetic information

CardioFamily aims to create a longitudinal family-action layer.

## Initial User

Inherited cardiovascular disease clinician / cardiogenetics team.

## Initial Disease

Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM).

## Primary Product Question

Who in this family needs my attention now?

## Initial Differentiation

CardioFamily is not primarily:
- a pedigree drawing tool
- a generic genomics record
- a variant classifier
- a genetic counselling chatbot

The initial differentiation is connecting:

family relationships
+ genetics
+ phenotype
+ surveillance
+ actions
+ reclassification

into one longitudinal family workflow.

## Key POC Capability

When relevant information changes, CardioFamily demonstrates which members of
the family may require clinician review and why.
